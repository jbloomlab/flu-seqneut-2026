## HA mutations between the 2025-2026 (J.2) and 2026-2027 (K) H3N2 vaccine strains

Structure of H3N2 HA ([PDB 8FAW](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8FAW)) with red indicating every site that differs between
2025-2026 vaccine strain (A/District Of Columbia/27/2023, subclade J.2)
and the 2026-2027 vaccine strain (A/Darwin/1415/2025, subclade K).
Sites that did not change shown in gray, and the sialic-acid receptor analogue is shown in black.

They differ at 10 sites:
  - Three mutations in antigenic region A: T135K, S144N, N145S
  - Three mutations in antigenic region B: N158D, I160K, K189R
  - One additional HA1 mutation outside the defined antigenic regions: Q173R
  - One HA2 mutation: S49N
  - Two mutations at sites not in the structure [PDB 8FAW](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8FAW) shown here: K2N and T328A

The HA sequences for the two strains are [PV280355](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PV280355) and
[PX422923](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PX422923).
