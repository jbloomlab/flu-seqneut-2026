## Classically defined antigenic regions of influenza H1 HA

Uncleaved HA from influenza A/Victoria/2570/2019, the egg-derived A(H1N1)pdm09 component of
the 2021 vaccine ([PDB 9GSP](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9GSP)). HA1 is
colored by the classical antigenic region each site belongs to, taken from Table 2 of
[Wilson et al. (2015), *Virology* 485:252-62](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5737639/),
which carries the regions Caton and colleagues mapped on A/PR/8/34 into the numbering of the
2009 pandemic lineage:

- **indigo** — site Sa, 13 sites: 124, 125, 153-157, 159-164
- **cyan** — site Sb, 12 sites: 184-195
- **green** — site Ca1, 11 sites: 166-170, 203-205, 235-237
- **purple** — site Ca2, 8 sites: 137-142, 221, 222
- **teal** — site Cb, 6 sites: 70-75
- **light gray** — HA1, not in a defined antigenic region
- **mid gray** — HA2

Sites are numbered in HA1 numbering, and all 50 of them are modeled in 9GSP. The regions
are disjoint, so every colored site belongs to exactly one of them.

Every residue carries its site in HA1 or HA2 numbering — `192_HA1`, `14_HA2` — shown on
mouseover.

Glycans are hidden.
