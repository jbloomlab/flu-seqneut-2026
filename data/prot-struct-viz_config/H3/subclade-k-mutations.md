## Subclade K mutations (relative to 2025-2026 vaccine)
Structure of H3N2 HA ([PDB 8FAW](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8FAW)) with red indicating every site that differs between
the 2025-2026 vaccine strain (A/District Of Columbia/27/2023, subclade J.2:S145N)
and the 2026-2027 vaccine strain (A/Darwin/1415/2025, subclade K).

Subclade K and J.2:S145N differ at 10 sites:

 - Three mutations in antigenic region A: T135K, S144N, N145S
 - Three mutations in antigenic region B: N158D, I160K, K189R
 - One additional HA1 mutation outside the defined antigenic regions: Q173R
 - One HA2 mutation: S49N
 - Two mutations at sites not in the structure shown here: K2N and T328A
