## Classically defined antigenic regions of influenza H3 HA

HA from influenza A/Victoria/22/2020 (H3N2) bound to **LSTc**, an analogue of
the alpha-2,6 sialylated receptor ([PDB 8FAW](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8FAW)). HA1 is
colored by the classical antigenic region each site belongs to, taken from Table 2 of
[Stray & Pittman (2012), *Virology Journal* 9:91](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3499391/):

- **indigo** — site A, 22 sites: 121-127, 129, 131-138, 140, 142-146
- **cyan** — site B, 19 sites: 155-160, 186, 188-190, 192-194, 196-199, 246, 247
- **green** — site C, 9 sites: 49, 50, 53, 54, 271, 273, 275, 276, 278
- **purple** — site D, 20 sites: 167, 201-207, 214, 216-220, 222, 223, 225-227, 242
- **teal** — site E, 12 sites: 62, 63, 75, 78-83, 91, 92, 94
- **light gray** — HA1, not in a defined antigenic region
- **mid gray** — HA2
- **black** — the LSTc receptor analogue

Sites are numbered in HA1 numbering, and all 82 of them are modeled in 8FAW. The regions
are disjoint, so every colored site belongs to exactly one of them.

Glycans are hidden.
