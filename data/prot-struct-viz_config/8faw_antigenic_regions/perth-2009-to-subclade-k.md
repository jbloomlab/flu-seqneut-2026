## HA mutations between a 2009 and 2025 H3N2 strain

Structure of H3N2 HA ([PDB 8FAW](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8FAW)) with red indicating every site that differs between
A/Perth/16/2009 (the H3N2 component of the 2010-2011 vaccine)
and the subclade K strain
A/Darwin/1415/2025 (the H3N2 component of the 2026-2027 vaccine).
Sites that did not change shown in gray, and the sialic-acid receptor analogue is shown in black.

27 of the 43 ectodomain mutations are at sites in the classically defined antigenic regions: eight
in site A, nine in site B, four in site C, two in site D, four in site E. All 27 are among the
40 at sites in the structure shown here.

The HA sequences for the two strains are [GQ293081](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/GQ293081) and
[PX422923](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PX422923).
